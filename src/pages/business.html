Six number levers that move revenue

💲

Price endings

Endings of 8, 88, 68 and 168 are standard in Chinese retail; 4 and 250 are avoided.

📦

Pack & bundle sizes

Sets of 2, 6, 8 and 9 feel generous; sets of 4 feel wrong as gifts.

📱

Phone & WhatsApp lines

A memorable 8-rich hotline boosts recall and signals an established business.

🏢

Addresses & units

Unit and floor numbers without 4 are easier to sell or lease to Chinese buyers.

📅

Launch dates

Openings on 8s, 18s and 28s, and outside Ghost Month, are widely preferred.

🧧

Promotions & gifts

Red-envelope promos with 8/88/888 values and 520/1314 campaigns for couples.
