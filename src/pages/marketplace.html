Marketplace FAQ

What fees do you charge?

Valuations are free. On successful brokered sales we charge a success commission agreed in writing before any work begins. No sale, no fee.

How is payment protected?

We use licensed third-party escrow services; the seller is paid only after the buyer confirms transfer.

Can license plates be transferred?

It depends on your jurisdiction. Some regions allow private transfer, others sell only through official auctions. We only facilitate transfers that are legal where you live.

How are values estimated?

By length, digit meanings (8/6/9 up, 4 down), patterns, comparable public sales and demand from Chinese-speaking buyers. Valuations are estimates, not guarantees.
