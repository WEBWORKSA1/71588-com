What makes a date auspicious?

  • Digits: dates containing 8, 6 or 9 (e.g. 8/8, 6/18, 9/9) are popular for weddings and openings; 4s are avoided.
  • Lunar days 8, 18 and 28 echo 发 (prosper) and are favoured for business starts.
  • The 7th lunar month (Ghost Month) is traditionally avoided for weddings, moves and launches.
  • Lunar New Year period (first 15 days of the 1st lunar month) is associated with fresh starts.
