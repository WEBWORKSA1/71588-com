About 71588.com

起·要我发发 — we help people and businesses use the language of numbers with confidence.

Our mission

Numbers carry meaning in Chinese culture — in phone numbers, prices, addresses, wedding dates and red envelopes. That meaning shapes real decisions and real money. Yet most explanations online are scattered, shallow or inaccurate.

71588.com brings it together: free interactive tools, a searchable dictionary, sourced guides and practical services for individuals and businesses who want to get numbers right.

What we believe

  • Culture, not superstition-selling. We explain traditions and their economic impact; we never promise luck, wealth or outcomes.
  • Sources over stories. Where we cite prices, research or history, we link to the source.
  • Free first. Our core tools stay free, funded by ads, sponsors and readers.
  • Respect. Regional readings differ (Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien). We note differences instead of flattening them.

How we make money

Advertising (including Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, affiliate partnerships, optional paid consultations, marketplace commissions and reader support. Editorial content is never for sale.
