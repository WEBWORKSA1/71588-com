Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

What we collect

  • Information you submit in forms (name, email, optional phone, messages, numbers you ask us to review). Form messages are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form relay.
  • Tool inputs (numbers, dates) are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Your last analyzed number and theme choice may be saved in your browser's local storage.
  • Usage data via cookies and similar technologies if analytics is enabled and you accept cookies.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

Embedded videos

Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and load only after you click play.

Payments

Donations are processed by PayPal or other payment providers; we never see or store your card details.

How we use information

To reply to you, deliver requested blueprints, reports and newsletters, run contests, process applications and improve the site. We never sell your personal information.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data, or unsubscribe at any time via our contact form. Residents of the EU/UK (GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA), Canada (PIPEDA) and other regions have rights under local law, which we honour.

Retention & security

We keep inquiries only as long as needed for the purpose collected, and use HTTPS throughout.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and contests are for adults 18+.
