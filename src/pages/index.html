Frequently asked

What is the luckiest number in Chinese culture?

8 (八, bā) — it sounds like 发 (fā), "to prosper". Combinations such as 88, 168 ("prosperity all the way") and 518 ("I will prosper") are prized in phone numbers, plates and prices.

Why is 4 unlucky?

四 (sì) sounds like 死 (sǐ), "death". Many buildings skip 4th floors and many buyers pay less for numbers containing 4. Full guide →

Are the scores scientific?

No. Our scores translate widely-held cultural associations into a simple number so you can compare options. They're for culture, fun and marketing insight — not predictions.
