How the score works

Each digit carries a traditional association drawn from how it sounds in Mandarin and Cantonese — 8 (发, prosper), 6 (顺, smooth), 9 (久, lasting) score high; 4 (死, death) scores low. We then look for well-known combinations (168, 518, 520, 1314, 250, 14…), give extra weight to the final digit, and reward patterns such as repeats, rising sequences and palindromes.

Honest note: this is a cultural-meaning tool, not fortune-telling. Use it for naming, pricing, gifting and marketing insight — and for fun.

Tips by number type

  • Phone numbers: the last four digits carry most of the value. Endings like 8888, 1688, 6688 and 5188 are prized.
  • License plates: shorter plates and repeated 8/6/9 dominate auction records.
  • Addresses & floors: avoid 4, 14, 24 and 44 if you plan to resell to Chinese-speaking buyers.
  • Prices: end in 8, 88, 68 or 168; avoid 4 and 250. Use the Lucky Price Finder.
