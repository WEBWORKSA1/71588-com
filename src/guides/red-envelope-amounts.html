Red Envelope Amounts 红包

The number inside matters as much as the gesture.

Golden rules

  • Even amounts for happy occasions (weddings, New Year); odd amounts are traditionally associated with funerals.
  • Include 8s, 6s or 9s where you can: 88, 168, 188, 288, 888.
  • Never include 4 — 40, 400, 44 are the worst choices.
  • Use new, crisp bills for paper envelopes.
  • Give and receive with both hands, and don't open the envelope in front of the giver.

Digital hongbao

WeChat and Alipay made digital red envelopes mainstream. WeChat's standard single-envelope cap is ¥200, and on special days like May 20 it has been temporarily raised to ¥520 so users can send the symbolic amount. (source)

Never give

  • Anything containing 4.
  • 250 (二百五, "fool").
  • Odd amounts at weddings.
  • Coins or old, torn notes.
