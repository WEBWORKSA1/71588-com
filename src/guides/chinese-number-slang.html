Chinese Number Slang Decoded

On the Chinese internet, numbers are a language of their own.

Love & affection

CodeReads asMeaning
520我爱你 wǒ ài nǐI love you
521我愿意I'm willing / I do
1314一生一世For a lifetime
5201314我爱你一生一世I love you forever
530我想你I miss you
3344生生世世Life after life

Praise, laughter & goodbyes

CodeReads asMeaning
666溜溜溜Awesome, slick, skilled
233哈哈哈LOL (from an old forum emoticon code)
88 / 886拜拜(了)Bye-bye
555呜呜呜Crying

Insults & things to avoid

CodeReads asMeaning
250二百五Idiot, fool
748去死吧Go die
7456气死我了I'm furious

Why it matters for brands

A price, coupon code or campaign hashtag can accidentally spell something rude — or can be a clever wink. Run codes through the Analyzer before launch.
