# Competitor & Best-Practice Audit (39 sites)

Sites were fetched on 2026-09-27. Where a site blocked access, a similar site was audited instead.

## Culture, number meaning and astrology sites
1. **TravelChinaGuide:** a long lucky-numbers explainer and a zodiac lucky-number table.
2. **China Highlights:** grades numbers into tiers, and uses its content to collect trip-booking leads.
3. **YourChineseAstrology:** has a date picker, a Q&A section with over 20,000 questions, and sells paid reports.
4. **ChineseZodiac.com:** offers a Four Pillars calculator and runs year-branded hubs.
5. **Astrology.com:** has accounts, a newsletter, premium readings and a dark/light mode toggle.
6. **Astrotheme:** substituted for Astro-Seek, which blocked access. It has many calculators and a store.
7. **Horoscope.com:** puts its newsletter in the menu and runs a "$1 reading" upsell.
8. **Cafe Astrology:** has free chart interpretations and an advice column with paid tiers.
9. **Numerology.com:** its main way of collecting emails is "Get your daily number" by email.
10. **Numerologist.com:** a quiz-funnel lead magnet, plus pages targeting angel-number searches.
11. **Feng Shui Nexus:** has a calculator in the hero section and an ask-an-expert form.
12. **World of Feng Shui:** has a magazine, calculators, a directory of consultants and a store.
13. **timeanddate.com:** date calculators, and an ad-free tier as a revenue source.
14. **MandarinTools:** substituted for chinesenames.org. It has a name generator.
15. **Mandarin Blueprint:** runs a lead quiz from its blog and has a table of contents on long posts.
16. **ChineseClass101:** calls to action for a free trial throughout the site.
17. **99designs:** runs contests: write a brief, receive entries, pick a winner.
18. **GoFundMe:** donation trust signals and a guarantee.
19. **Buy Me a Coffee:** preset amounts, a wall of supporters and memberships.

30. **DNJournal:** a sales report paid for by sponsor banners.
31. **China Daily Culture:** themed culture hubs.
32. **Sixth Tone:** a minimal newsletter signup and author bylines.
33. **What's on Weibo:** weekly trend explainers.
34. **Dragon Trail:** a China marketing agency with gated e-books and client logos.
35. **HubSpot:** two calls to action side by side, and free tools as lead magnets.
36. **NeilPatel.com:** a hero with a single input field and a form that qualifies leads by budget.
37. **Kaggle and Product Hunt:** contest tabs, leaderboards and badges.
38. **Wellfound:** job cards showing salary and a signal for how quickly the employer responds.
39. **Ko-fi, plus "advertise with us" pages collected by Kevel and The Ridge:** support tiers, and media-kit request forms.

## Features adopted on 71588.com
- **Tools:**
  - A tool in the hero section.
  - Number analyzer (phone, plate, address, price and domain modes).
  - Dictionary with category filters.
  - Generator and price finder.
  - Zodiac tools: lunar-accurate year and compatibility.
  - Auspicious-date calendar.
  - Number of the day.
- **Lead generation:**
  - Free result first, then an email gate for the full report.
  - A dedicated multi-step Blueprint funnel with budget qualification.
  - Exit-intent modal.
  - Newsletter on every page.
- **Marketplace:**
  - Buy request with alerts, sell/valuation form, escrow and a 4-step "how it works".
- **Advertising:**
  - Packages with starting prices, a media kit request form and an ad-standards policy.
- **Donations:**
  - Donations in lucky amounts, monthly tiers, a progress bar and a supporter wall.
- **Contests:**
  - Countdown, tabs (overview, prizes, rules, timeline, judging, winners), entry form and sponsor slots.
- **Careers:**
  - Role cards, contributor call and application form.
- **Content and discovery:**
  - Guides with tables of contents and sources.
  - Video hub with a click-to-load player.
  - FAQ with schema markup.
  - Dark mode.
