# 71588: Cultural and Economic Research

## Mandarin reading
**起 · 要我发发**: "Rise: I want to prosper, and prosper again."

| Digit | Character | Sounds like | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 七 qī | 起 rise · 齐 together · Qixi romance | Mixed: the 7th lunar month is Ghost Month |
| 1 | 一 / 幺 yāo | 要 want, will (as in 518 我要发) | Neutral + |
| 5 | 五 wǔ | 我 me · Five Elements | Depends on context |
| 8 | 八 bā | 发 prosper | Strongest positive |
| 8 | 八 bā | 发发 double prosperity · 88 = "bye-bye" · looks like 囍 | Strong positive |

- **Strengths:**
  - It builds on the documented 518 phrase (我要发, "I will prosper").
  - It ends in 88.
  - It contains no 4, the number most buyers mark down.
- **Caveats:**
  - Cantonese speakers may hear 5 as 唔 ("not"), and 7 as vulgar slang.
  - 7-15 recalls the Ghost Festival, held on the 15th day of the 7th lunar month.
  - **Positioning:** 71588 works best as a Mandarin story about rising and prospering.

## Economic evidence
- **Phone number 8888-8888** (Chengdu, 2003): ¥2.33M, about US$280K, paid by Sichuan Airlines. The same auction's 100 numbers raised ¥7.13M. ([China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/en/doc/2003-08/19/content_256178.htm))
- **Hong Kong plates:**
  - "18": HK$16.5M (2008)
  - "28": HK$18.1M (2016)
  - "W": HK$26M (2021)

  ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/style/article/hong-kong-auctioned-vanity-car-plates-intl-hnk/index.html))
- **Chinese IPO prices, 1999–2009:**
  - 19.2% ended in 8, versus 3.3% ending in 4.
  - Where managers set prices freely, 22.5% ended in 8.

  ([Sun & Schneider](https://www.cfrn.com.cn/uploads/fileupload/2947/paper/1005171245516107.pdf))
- **Chengdu property:**
  - Resale units on floors ending in 8 fetched about ¥235/m² more.
  - New units on those floors sold 6.9 days faster.

  ([J. Comparative Economics 2014](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jcecon/v42y2014i1p109-117.html))
- **Advertised prices:**
  - About 40% end in 8 in Chinese and Taiwanese ads, versus 1.4% ending in 4.
  - The share ending in 8 is 31.1% in Singapore.
- **Beijing 2008 Olympics:** the Games opened at 8:08 pm on 08-08-08, and weddings surged that day.
- **WeChat red envelopes:** the cap was temporarily raised to ¥520 for 520 Day.
- **5-digit numeric .com domains:**
  - The wholesale floor is roughly $70–$500, depending on the year.
  - Numbers with a pattern, or that are 8-heavy and 4-free, sell in the low four figures or more.
